Our Story

BUILT FOR
THE GAME

Kickoff was born out of a simple frustration: organising local soccer matches shouldn't require a spreadsheet degree. Manual brackets, WhatsApp score updates, and forgotten tallies — we've all been there.

Whether you're running a weekend kickabout with friends or a competitive local league, our goal is to give you professional-grade tools that feel effortless to use. Get on the pitch. We'll handle the rest.
